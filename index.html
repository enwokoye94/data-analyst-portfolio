About Me

Hi, I'm Ekene, a data analyst specializing in sales operations and business intelligence: the reporting, forecasting, and data quality work revenue teams run on.

I've spent 5+ years in data and operations roles, including Data Consultant (contract) at Summit Midstream, Order Specialist (remote, contract) at AT&T via Pinnacle Group, and Operations Analyst at Constellation Energy. My toolkit is Power BI and DAX, SQL, Python, and advanced Excel, plus Salesforce and Oracle ERP. B.S. Mechanical Engineering, University of New Hampshire; Project Management certified.
